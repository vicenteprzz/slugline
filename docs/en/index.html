FAQ

My Mac says it can't check the app

That's expected: Slugline doesn't carry Apple's developer signature yet. It isn't a virus, and it only happens the first time.

  1. If the browser blocks the download, choose Keep in the downloads list (or download it with Safari).
  2. Drag Slugline to Applications, open it once and close the warning.
  3. In System Settings → Privacy & Security, at the very bottom, click Open Anyway.
Windows says it protected my PC

That's expected: Slugline doesn't carry a developer signature yet. It isn't a virus, and it only happens the first time.

  1. If the browser warns about the download, choose Keep.
  2. Open Slugline-Setup.exe. In the blue warning, click More info and then Run anyway.
  3. Follow the installer. It doesn't ask for an administrator password.
Do I need AI?

No. Without AI the app does everything. With your own Anthropic API key, the breakdown comes out fuller, every scene gets a summary and you can ask questions about the script. You pay for that on your own account, as you use it.

How does the licence work?

You get a key by email, paste it into Settings and that's it. No accounts, no hidden subscriptions.

Is the budget exact?

The quantities are, because they come from the script and the schedule. The rates are a starting point, and what can't be known from a script (effects, fees) is marked to be quoted.

Does it run on Windows?

Yes, on Windows 10 and 11. It's the same app and it reads scanned PDFs too. If anything goes wrong, write to me and I'll fix it.

Which languages?

It reads scripts in English, Spanish and Catalan, and the app and its PDFs come in all three: pick one in Settings.
