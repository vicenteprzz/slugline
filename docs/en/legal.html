Privacy

Data controller: Vicente Pérez Pourtau, hola@getslugline.com.

The app

  • Your scripts and projects stay on your computer. Slugline has no server: it doesn't upload them, nobody reads them, and there's no account to create.
  • It sends no usage data. The app doesn't track what you do or who you are.
  • It checks for a new version by asking GitHub at most twice a day. The request carries only the app's version number, and GitHub sees the connection like any visit (its policy). You can turn it off with the environment variable SLUGLINE_NO_UPDATE_CHECK=1.
  • "Report a problem" opens your email program with a message that includes the Slugline and system versions. Nothing is sent until you send it.
  • The licence is checked offline. The key carries the name you gave when you bought it, and is stored only on your computer.
  • AI is optional. If you turn it on with your own Anthropic key, the script's text is sent to Anthropic for analysis, under their terms. Without a key, nothing leaves your computer.

The website

  • We use no cookies. That's why you see no cookie banner.
  • We count visits and downloads with GoatCounter, with no cookies and without storing your IP address or anything that identifies you: only how often each page is seen, from which country and on what kind of computer.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and downloads come from GitHub, which logs connections like any server (its policy).
  • The YouTube video only loads if you click "Watch the demo", in its no-cookie version. Until then, YouTube receives nothing.
  • Fonts are served from this site, not from Google.
